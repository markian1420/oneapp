Why these are typed in by hand

No Philippine chain publishes promos in any machine-readable form. The brand pages are images with the terms baked into the graphic and no validity dates in text, and the deal blogs grant no reuse licence. So this is a notebook, not a feed — and the field that matters most is the one nobody publishes: when it ends. A promo tracker showing expired offers is worse than no tracker at all.
