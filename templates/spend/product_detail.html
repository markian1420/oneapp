Why this is not a price search

No Philippine retailer publishes product prices in any machine-readable form. Shopee and Lazada expose seller-side APIs only, and the brands' own stores are client-rendered with no product schema — Salomon PH's page carries a single Organization block and prices as loose text not tied to any model. Scraping it would break on the next redesign and, worse, would sometimes report the wrong shoe's price with complete confidence.

So this remembers what you find, and does the part software is good at: keeping track of which seller is which, and noticing when a domain is wearing a brand's name without being the brand.
