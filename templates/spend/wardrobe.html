Why this and not a price map

Clothing is the one category where "cheapest nearby" means almost nothing. You buy a specific shirt once, it is not interchangeable with another shirt, and no Philippine retailer publishes shelf prices anyway. What does change buying habits is cost per wear: a ₱3,000 jacket worn 100 times costs ₱30 a wear, while a ₱600 shirt worn twice costs ₱300.
