Nothing here is live. Philippine pump prices move once a week on a Tuesday, the DA publishes its commodity index once a weekday and often late in the afternoon, and banks add promos when they feel like it. So rather than claim real time, every number carries its own date and every feed states the cadence it genuinely publishes on.

One command refreshes the lot: python manage.py refresh_all

How this works

Rolling medians, month-on-month deltas and simple comparisons — nothing you could not check by hand. At the volumes a personal budget produces, anything opaque would be fitting noise and presenting it with a straight face. Every line says how many observations it rests on, so a thin one reads as thin instead of arriving with the same authority as a solid one.
