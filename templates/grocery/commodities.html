Where these come from

The Department of Agriculture publishes a daily prevailing retail price for NCR, averaged across 33 wet markets. It is the whole region, not one market — so treat it as the going rate to check a stall against, not as the price you will be quoted. Anything you buy yourself is worth more than this, and gets logged separately.
