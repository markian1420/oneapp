No prices here yet

Fuel is the only kind of place the app can resolve a price for, because the DOE publishes a weekly advisory to fall back on. No Philippine supermarket or shop publishes shelf prices, so what a basket costs here is something only a receipt can say — and basket logging is not built yet.

For produce and meat, the commodity prices screen carries the DA daily index for NCR, which is a fair reference for what a stall should be asking.
